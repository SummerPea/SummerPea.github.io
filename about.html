More About Me

Background

I am Haolin Zhang, a student interested in creative writing and digital projects. I use this portfolio to collect the novels, games, and other experiments I am working on.

What I enjoy making

I like combining stories and technology: writing fictional worlds, exploring game ideas, and learning through small apps and experiments. Each project is a chance to practice a different part of the creative process.

Goals

My goal is to finish and share more complete works while documenting how they develop. This website will grow as new projects become ready to publish.

Contact

This portfolio is the best place to follow my public work for now. A public contact or social link will be added here when there is a channel I can maintain reliably.
